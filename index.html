WHO WE ARE

About Matlas Solutions

Matlas Solutions is a Virginia-based technical staffing and IT services company focused on connecting federal, state, local, and defense organizations with the talent they need to get the mission done.

We place qualified IT and engineering professionals on short-term projects and long-term contracts, backed by enterprise IT support, infrastructure services, and systems engineering designed to improve operational efficiency and help our customers achieve their mission objectives.

As a registered government contractor, Matlas Solutions is committed to building trusted partnerships and delivering responsive, high-quality staffing and technology services that meet the evolving needs of government organizations.
